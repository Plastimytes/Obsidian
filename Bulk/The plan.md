### Overview:

- **Duration**: 8 weeks
- **Frequency**: 4-5 days per week
- **Focus**: Progressive overload (gradually increasing volume or intensity)
- **Goal**: Build muscle mass (hypertrophy) and strength through compound movements and targeted isolation exercises
### Program Structure

- **Weeks 1-4**: Foundation Phase (Focus on building strength and mastering form)
- **Weeks 5-8**: Intensity Phase (Increase volume and weight for hypertrophy)
### Weekly Breakdown

- **Day 1**: Upper Body (Push: Chest, Shoulders, Triceps)
- **Day 2**: Lower Body (Quads, Hamstrings, Glutes)
- **Day 3**: Rest or Active Recovery (light cardio, stretching)
- **Day 4**: Upper Body (Pull: Back, Biceps)
- **Day 5**: Lower Body (Deadlift Focus, Glutes, Hamstrings)
- **Day 6**: Optional Rest or Active Recovery
- **Day 7**: Rest
### **Weeks 1-4: Foundation Phase**

#### Day 1: Upper Body (Push)

1. **Barbell Bench Press**: 4 sets x 6-8 reps
2. **Dumbbell Shoulder Press**: 4 sets x 8-10 reps
3. **Incline Dumbbell Press**: 3 sets x 8-10 reps
4. **Lateral Raises**: 3 sets x 12-15 reps
5. **Tricep Dips**: 3 sets x 8-10 reps (weighted if possible)
6. **Tricep Pushdowns**: 3 sets x 10-12 reps
#### Day 2: Lower Body (Quads, Hamstrings, Glutes)

1. **Barbell Back Squat**: 4 sets x 6-8 reps
2. **Leg Press**: 3 sets x 8-10 reps
3. **Walking Lunges**: 3 sets x 12 reps per leg
4. **Romanian Deadlifts**: 3 sets x 8-10 reps
5. **Leg Extensions**: 3 sets x 12-15 reps
6. **Leg Curls**: 3 sets x 12-15 reps
#### Day 4: Upper Body (Pull)

1. **Pull-Ups (or Lat Pulldowns)**: 4 sets x 6-8 reps
2. **Barbell Bent-Over Rows**: 4 sets x 6-8 reps
3. **Seated Cable Rows**: 3 sets x 8-10 reps
4. **Dumbbell Bicep Curls**: 3 sets x 10-12 reps
5. **Barbell or Hammer Curls**: 3 sets x 10-12 reps
6. **Face Pulls**: 3 sets x 12-15 reps
#### Day 5: Lower Body (Deadlift Focus)

1. **Deadlifts**: 4 sets x 5-6 reps
2. **Bulgarian Split Squats**: 3 sets x 8-10 reps per leg
3. **Hip Thrusts**: 4 sets x 8-10 reps
4. **Glute Bridges**: 3 sets x 12-15 reps
5. **Calf Raises**: 3 sets x 12-15 reps
### **Weeks 5-8: Intensity Phase**

In these weeks, focus on increasing weight, reps, and sets to continue progressing. You can also decrease rest time slightly to increase intensity.

#### Day 1: Upper Body (Push)

1. **Barbell Bench Press**: 4 sets x 6-8 reps (increase weight from previous weeks)
2. **Overhead Dumbbell Press**: 4 sets x 8-10 reps (increase reps or weight)
3. **Incline Dumbbell Press**: 4 sets x 8-10 reps
4. **Lateral Raises**: 3 sets x 12-15 reps
5. **Tricep Dips (weighted)**: 4 sets x 6-8 reps
6. **Skull Crushers**: 3 sets x 10-12 reps
#### Day 2: Lower Body (Quads, Hamstrings, Glutes)

1. **Barbell Squat**: 4 sets x 6-8 reps (increase weight or reps)
2. **Hack Squat Machine**: 4 sets x 8-10 reps
3. **Walking Lunges**: 4 sets x 12 reps per leg (with added weight)
4. **Romanian Deadlifts**: 4 sets x 8-10 reps
5. **Leg Extensions**: 3 sets x 12-15 reps
6. **Leg Curls**: 3 sets x 12-15 reps
#### Day 4: Upper Body (Pull)

1. **Pull-Ups (weighted if possible)**: 4 sets x 6-8 reps
2. **Barbell Rows**: 4 sets x 6-8 reps
3. **T-Bar Rows**: 3 sets x 8-10 reps
4. **Dumbbell Bicep Curls**: 4 sets x 10-12 reps
5. **Preacher Curls**: 3 sets x 10-12 reps
6. **Face Pulls**: 3 sets x 12-15 reps
#### Day 5: Lower Body (Deadlift Focus)

1. **Deadlifts**: 4 sets x 4-6 reps (increase weight)
2. **Bulgarian Split Squats**: 4 sets x 8-10 reps per leg
3. **Hip Thrusts**: 4 sets x 8-10 reps (increase weight)
4. **Glute Bridges**: 4 sets x 12-15 reps
5. **Standing Calf Raises**: 4 sets x 12-15 reps

### **Progressive Overload Guidelines**

- **Increase Weight**: Each week, aim to increase the weight on each exercise by 2.5-5% (if you can maintain proper form).
- **Increase Reps**: Once you hit the upper end of the rep range (e.g., 8-10 reps), increase the weight slightly and start again at the lower end of the rep range.
- **Increase Sets**: If you feel capable, increase the number of sets for an exercise, especially for larger muscle groups (e.g., squats, deadlifts).
- **Rest Time**: Gradually decrease rest time from 90 seconds to 60 seconds between sets to increase intensity.
### Example of Progression:

- **Week 1**: Barbell Bench Press 4 sets x 6 reps at 135 lbs
- **Week 2**: Barbell Bench Press 4 sets x 7 reps at 140 lbs
- **Week 3**: Barbell Bench Press 4 sets x 8 reps at 145 lbs
- **Week 4**: Barbell Bench Press 4 sets x 8 reps at 150 lbs
### Nutrition Tips for Bulking:

- **Caloric Surplus**: Aim for a slight caloric surplus (250-500 calories above maintenance) to gain muscle without excessive fat.
- **Macronutrients**: Focus on getting a balanced intake of protein (1.2-2.0 grams per kg of body weight), healthy fats, and carbohydrates.
- **Hydration**: Drink plenty of water to stay hydrated, especially when lifting heavier weights.
