Title: Automated lighting for UCU lecture rooms.(Motion sensors, circuit breakers)

Problem statement: Lights in lecture rooms at UCU are constantly left on but not in use which leads to higher power consumptions creating useless expenses which could be used in more pressing university matters, energy wastage and inefficient resource management as there is a higher rate of spoilage in bulbs and electrical wires. In addition a member of the security staff have to travel all over the school to turn off lights wasting his skills and creating another cost for the university through his salary and allowances.

This problem statement highlights the need for a more efficient approach to address electricity wastage within the university through use of motion sensors and circuit breakers.

Methodology: Obtaining the electrical wiring architectural plans for UCU buildings that will be affected.
Questionnaires to interview students and staff on how lecture rooms are used and who uses them the most and at what times. Suggestions on best practices that can best practice that can be implemented in future upgrades.
A test room to first be used to carryout a test run for this implementation and experiment to figure out bugs with the system to make it more efficient
After the design is determined it should rolled out on a large scale test.

Lights will turn off by themselves at a given time in the night.
