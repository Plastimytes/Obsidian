**1. Engine Block**

- **Material:** Typically cast iron or aluminum alloy.
- **Construction:**
    - **Cylinder bores:** Precision-machined holes that house the pistons.
    - **Main bearing journals:** Support the crankshaft.
    - **Water jackets:** Channels for coolant circulation.
    - **Oil galleries:** Channels for oil distribution.
    - **Deck surface:** The top surface where the cylinder head mounts.
    - **Main bearing caps:** Bolt-on covers that secure the main bearings.
    - **Studs and nuts:** High-strength fasteners that secure the cylinder head.
    - **Dowels:** Align the cylinder head with the block.
    - **Freeze plugs:** (Also known as core plugs) Small, removable plugs that seal off water jacket openings during manufacturing.

**2. Cylinder Head**

- **Material:** Usually aluminum alloy.
- **Construction:**
    - **Combustion chambers:** Shaped to optimize the combustion process.
    - **Valve seats:** Precision-machined surfaces that support the valves.
    - **Valve guides:** Guide the vertical movement of the valves.
    - **Intake and exhaust ports:** Channels for airflow.
    - **Water jackets:** Channels for coolant circulation.
    - **Spark plug holes:** (In gasoline engines)
    - **Fuel injector holes:** (In fuel-injected engines)
    - **Head gasket surface:** Precision-machined surface for sealing against the block.
    - **Rocker arm supports:** (In overhead valve engines) Support the rocker arms.

**3. Pistons**

- **Material:** Aluminum alloy.
- **Construction:**
    - **Piston crown:** The top surface, often shaped for optimal combustion.
    - **Piston rings:**
        - **Compression rings:** Seal the combustion chamber and prevent blow-by.
        - **Oil rings:** Control oil consumption.
    - **Piston skirt:** The lower portion of the piston, reduces friction.
    - **Piston pin bores:** Holes that accept the piston pin.
    - **Wrist pin:** (Another term for piston pin) Connects the piston to the connecting rod.

**4. Connecting Rods**

- **Material:** Forged steel or aluminum alloys.
- **Construction:**
    - **Connecting rod body:** Transmits force from the piston to the crankshaft.
    - **Big end:** The end that connects to the crankshaft.
    - **Small end:** The end that connects to the piston.
    - **Connecting rod bearings:**
        - **Big end bearing:** Reduces friction at the crankshaft.
        - **Small end bearing:** Reduces friction at the piston pin.
    - **Connecting rod bolts:** High-strength bolts that secure the rod to the piston and crankshaft.

**5. Crankshaft**

- **Material:** Forged steel.
- **Construction:**
    - **Main journals:** Cylindrical surfaces that support the crankshaft in the main bearings.
    - **Crank pins:** Connect the connecting rods to the crankshaft.
    - **Counterweights:** Balance the rotating mass of the crankshaft.
    - **Thrust surfaces:** Prevent axial movement of the crankshaft.
    - **Keyways:** Secure the flywheel and crankshaft pulley.

**6. Camshaft**

- **Material:** Cast iron or steel.
- **Construction:**
    - **Camshaft lobes:** Raise and lower the valves.
    - **Camshaft journals:** Support the camshaft in the cylinder head.
    - **Timing gears/belt/chain:** Drive the camshaft from the crankshaft.

**7. Valves**

- **Material:** Stainless steel or other heat-resistant alloys.
- **Construction:**
    - **Valve head:** The part that seals against the valve seat.
    - **Valve stem:** Connects the valve head to the valve spring.
    - **Valve guides:** Guide the vertical movement of the valves.
    - **Valve springs:** Return the valves to their closed position.
    - **Valve retainers:** Hold the valve springs in place.
    - **Valve seals:** Prevent oil from leaking past the valve stems.

**8. Fuel System (Gasoline Engines)**

- **Fuel Pump:**
    - **Electric fuel pump:** Powered by the vehicle's electrical system.
    - **Mechanical fuel pump:** Driven by the engine.
- **Fuel Injectors:**
    - **Single-point injection:** One injector for the entire engine.
    - **Multi-point injection:** One injector for each cylinder.
    - **Direct injection:** Inject fuel directly into the combustion chamber.
- **Fuel Lines:** High-pressure lines that deliver fuel to the injectors.
- **Fuel Filter:** Removes contaminants from the fuel.
- **Fuel Pressure Regulator:** Maintains consistent fuel pressure.
- **Fuel Rail:** A common fuel supply line for multiple injectors.

**9. Ignition System (Gasoline Engines)**

- **Ignition Coils:**
    - **Coil-on-plug:** One coil per spark plug.
    - **Wasted spark:** One coil for multiple cylinders.
- **Spark Plugs:**
    - **Center electrode:** Delivers the spark.
    - **Ground electrode:** Provides a path for the spark.
    - **Insulator:** Isolates the center electrode from the ground electrode.

**10. Cooling System**

- **Water Pump:**
    - **Impeller:** Pumps coolant through the engine.
    - **Pump housing:** Contains the impeller.
- **Radiator:**
    - **Radiator core:** Contains coolant passages and fins for heat dissipation.
    - **Radiator fans:** Force air through the radiator.
- **Thermostat:** Controls coolant flow to the radiator.
- **Coolant Hoses:** Connect the radiator, engine, and water pump.
- **Radiator Cap:** Maintains proper coolant pressure.

**11. Lubrication System**

- **Oil Pump:**
    - **Gear-type pump:** Common type of oil pump.
- **Oil Filter:**
    - **Full-flow filter:** Filters all of the engine's oil supply.
    - **Bypass filter:** Filters a portion of the oil supply.
- **Oil Pan:** Holds the engine oil.
- **Oil Pickup Tube:** Draws oil from the oil pan.
- **Oil Pressure Relief Valve:** Regulates oil pressure.

**12. Intake and Exhaust Systems**

- **Intake Manifold:**
    - **Runner length:** Affects engine performance at different RPMs.
    - **Intake ports:** Channels for air and fuel flow.
- **Throttle Body:** Controls the amount of air entering the engine.
- **Exhaust Manifold:**
    - **Primary tubes:** Collect exhaust gases from each cylinder.
    - **Collector:** Merges exhaust gases from multiple cylinders.
- **Catalytic Converter:** Reduces harmful emissions.
- **Muffler:** Reduces exhaust noise.
- **Resonator:** Further reduces exhaust noise.
