Human centered design is working on a project or solution while interacting and keeping end users in mind.

**Team formation**
 Innovation is a team sport.
 Best teams comprise of; A hacker to focus on product, A hustler to focus on the customer, An artist to focus on design.
 *Techie*-Builds the actual product
 *Designer*-Works on how it looks and focuses on the idea
 *Business person*-Finds right business model, Recognizes new trends and sees patterns
 
**What is key in project management?**
  Building something on time and on budget.

**Innovation process**
  User needs->idea->Business case->Innovation
  **Stages**
  Understand problem
  User
  Technology
  Ideation
  Prototype 
  Validate
  Pitch

Stage 1( *Understand problem*)
Main facts, insights, figures, statistics and data points of relevance.
When you don't understand the problem you are solving nothing. Spend time understanding the problem. Tasks at this stage include Reading, Discussing, Analyzing and Researching.

Stage 2(*User*)
Know your users. Identify, Analyze, Select, Choose your segment and challenge (Identify ,Analyze, Select, Describe).
Problem Selection-Focus is on Importance and Frequency. Problem must have a painful end result.
