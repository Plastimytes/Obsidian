Text blogs and Video blogs(Vlogs)

**Types of blogs**
  Academic blogs
  Share research on different topics

Niche Based Blogs
   Focus on a specific topic appealing to a targeted audience.

**How to write a blog post**
Step 1
 Chose a niche Subject.
 Defining Goals.
 Understanding your Audience.

Step 2: Structuring your blog
  Find the blog format.
  Title-Attention Grabbing headlines
  Using visuals.

Step 3 : Writing the blog
 SEO basics(Search Engine Optimization)- Use specific words to optimize your blogs.
 Engage the reader.
 Keep sentences concise and easy to read.
 Avoid jargon and complicated words.
 Prioritize editing and proof reading before publishing.

Step 4 : Enhancing and Publishing
  Formatting for readability.
  Compare Publishing platforms.
  Publishing Checklist.
  Proofread for errors, Check for plagiarism.
  Optimize technical SEO.

Step 5: Promoting the blog
  Sharing on Social Media
  Engaging with Readers
  Networking 
  Email Marketing- Build subscriber list and send newsletters.

Step 6: Evaluating Performance
 Using Analytics
 Use tools like Google Analytics to track: Traffic, bounce rate and time on page.
 Learn from feedback.
