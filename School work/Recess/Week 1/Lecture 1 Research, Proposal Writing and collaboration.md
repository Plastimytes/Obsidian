 Research-systemic process of gathering and analyzing information.
 Proposal-A formal document outlining a plan or idea for a given goa;
 Credible Sources- Reliable and trustworthy info. used in research.
 Data-Raw facts and figures collected for analysis. Primary and secondary data.
 Concept note-a brief doc. summarizing the main aspects of a research idea. It comes before the proposal. Contains problem statement, objectives etc.

  **Why research?**
  Informed decision making. Keeping your idea in line with the ideals of the company.
  Builds credibility and supports arguments.

 **Why Proposal writing?**
 Effective idea comm.
 Streamline research.
 Increases chances of securing approval or funding.

 Identifying Credible sources
 What makes a source credible?
 Authorship-Qualified and reputable author or org.
 Publication date- Recent and relevant info.(Debatable)
 References-Backed by citations and evidence.
 Bias-Neutral and objective content.
 Peer Review-Published in reputable journals.

 Credible: WHO reports, Nature journal articles.
 Non credible: Blogs, opinion pieces without citations.

 **Gathering Data**
 Primary Data:
 Firsthand collection. E.G: Surveys, Interviews, experiments.
 Secondary Data:
 Pre-existing data from books, journals, and reports. E.G: PubMed articles, govt statistics. 

 **Ethics in data collection:**
 Credit all sources to avoid plagiarism.
 Respect confidentiality in primary data collection.


 **Structuring a research proposal**
  Key sections:
   Title: Clear and concise
   Introduction /Problem Statement: Define the issue or opportunity.
  Objectives: SMART(Specific Measurable Achievable Realistic Time-bound) goals. 
   Example::
   General object: "Increasing recycling awareness"
   SMART: "Conduct 5 workshops over 3 months to educate 300 students on recycling"
  Methodology: How  you'll achieve objectives.
  Expected Outcomes: Highlight anticipated impact.
  Optional Sections:
 Budget and timeline
Concept note dev.
 A concise summary of the project idea.
 Structure:
  Title 
  Problem Statement
  Objectives
  Methodology 
  Expected outcomes
