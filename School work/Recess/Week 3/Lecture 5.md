**Pitching Practice**
  What is someone looking for?
  Goal: Sell your product, Sell your company, Sell Yourself(Team)
  Within a short time: 5mins pitching and 5 mins Q&A.(If you can't explain something simply, you don't understand it well enough)

Anatomy of a great pitch
 Hook(Either an example scenario or the actual problem with facts and figures)->Elevator pitch: Captures your audience immediately like a picture that speaks to the solution or problem. Maybe add a tag line that briefly describes what you are trying to achieve.
 
 Problem(Make your audience aware that there is a problem)
 
 Solution(Identify 5 keywords that apply to your value proposition only(Easy to use, fun, intelligent....))->Your Demo(We are solving this {problem} by providing {advantage} to help {target}...)
 
 Market size(How many people are you targeting)->Why is your product uniquely positioned to capture this market.
 
 Business Model->The solution must make money(We make money by charging {customer} to get {benefits}).
 
 Traction(This for people who have built something so focus on the plan)
 Competition-> Advantage over the competition, Use a table which represents your current competitors against you.(What makes you better?)
 Team
 The Ask
NB: The contacts should be at the last page
 The first 30 seconds decide whether people listen to you more.
 Key Things: Problem, solution and how it works

How it works
