**Types of voltage**

AC vs DC 
 AC-Used in household power and power outlets. Voltage fluctuates btwn +ve and -ve cycles
 DC-Used in computer components. Voltage remains constant in one direction

Electrical Safety tips
 Always turn off and unplug devices before opening them.
 Avoid working in wet or damp environments.
 Use insulated tools when handling electrical components.
 Dont touch exposed cicuits while power is on.
 Wear rubber soled shoes to reduce risk of electric shock
 Use current limiting devices (Fuses) in power circuits.

Introduction to a multimeter
 A tool used to measure electrical properties such as voltage, current and resistance
