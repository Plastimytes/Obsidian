1.A business Case refers to the comprehensive yet easy to understand reasons or justification for a proposal.
It should answer the following; Why are we doing this project?, What is it about? How much will it cost and how long will it take?
2.  The purpose of Strategic planning is to identify long term goals, strategies and resources. The four factors involved in the SWOT analysis are Strengths, Weaknesses, opportunities and threats.
3. Technical feasibility refers to technical resources needed to develop, purchase and install or operate a system like will a prototype be required while operational feasibility refers to the limit to which the proposed system will be used after it has been developed like whether customer will have a bad experience or will any information be more or less accessible.
4. Tangible benefits are those which are immediately felt after a system has been developed and implemented while intangible benefits are those that affect the organization but are not really felt by individuals.
5. 
Step 1: Understand the problem or opportunity
Step 2: Define the project scope and constraints. Set the specific boundaries or extent of the project.
Step 3:Perform fact finding that is analyzation of organizational charts, Interviews, Documentation review, Operation observation and user surveys.
Step 4:Evaluate feasibility
Step 5: Evaluate project development time and cost. After gathering cost and benefit data one should be able to prepare financial analysis and impact statements using spreadsheets and other decision support tools
Step 6: Presentation of results and recommendations to the managing body. With the first section being an overview of the report followed by a system request summary, a case for action, Project roles and time and cost estimates.
